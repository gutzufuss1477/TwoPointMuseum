[b]Two Point Museum QoL Trainer 1.0.0[/b]

Configurable QoL tweaks with a multilingual self-installing trainer.

[b]Installation[/b]
Extract the download and run [b]TPMQoLTrainer.exe[/b]. That's it.

The trainer automatically finds Two Point Museum, installs the tested official BepInEx 6 IL2CPP x64 build when needed, installs/updates the mod and creates the config. Existing compatible BepInEx installations and user settings are preserved.

[b]Main features[/b]
[list]
[*] Workshop, Training, Analysis, Veterinary and Expedition speed: 1x-20x
[*] Staff movement: 1x-5x
[*] Max knowledge options
[*] Applicant rank and clean training slots
[*] Expedition survey and quality controls
[*] Security coverage radius
[*] First Aid cures expedition illnesses
[*] Live config editing and Vanilla reset
[*] 15 trainer languages
[/list]

Staff movement preserves vanilla energy, fatigue, robot charging and movement-skill modifiers.

Tested with Two Point Museum 12.0.243206 and BepInEx 6.0.0-be.788.
